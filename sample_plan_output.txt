Multi-Year Planning (3-year rotation example):

- Year 2: Fruiting crops (tomatoes, peppers, cucumbers) in beds following legumes.

🏥 Disease Prevention & Management Guide

Common diseases for your crops in the Northeast region:

**Lettuce**:

  Downy Mildew:
    Risk Level in Northeast: 🟠 High
    Symptoms: Yellow angular spots on upper leaf surface
    Prevention:
      • Plant in full sun with good air flow
      • Avoid overhead watering
      • Space plants adequately
    Risk Factors: Cool moist weather, High humidity

  Bottom Rot:
    Risk Level in Northeast: 🟡 Moderate
    Symptoms: Brown rot on lower leaves touching soil
    Prevention:
      • Avoid overwatering
      • Mulch to prevent soil splash
      • Improve drainage
    Risk Factors: Excess moisture, Poor drainage

**General Disease Prevention Tips**:
  ✓ Water at base of plants, avoid wetting foliage
  ✓ Ensure proper plant spacing for air circulation
  ✓ Mulch to prevent soil splash onto leaves
  ✓ Remove diseased plant material immediately
  ✓ Practice crop rotation (3-4 year cycle)
  ✓ Sanitize tools between plants
  ✓ Choose disease-resistant varieties when available
  ✓ Don't work in garden when plants are wet
  ✓ Remove debris and weeds that harbor diseases
  ✓ Monitor plants weekly for early detection

**When to Act**:
  • Inspect plants weekly, especially after rain
  • Catch diseases early when treatment is most effective
  • Remove infected leaves/plants promptly
  • Apply organic treatments at first sign of issues
  • Contact extension office if unsure about identification
