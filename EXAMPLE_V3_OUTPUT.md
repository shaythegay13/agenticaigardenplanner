🌾 Cover Crop Recommendations

Improve Soil Health Between Growing Seasons:

Winter Rye:
  - Optimal planting window: Sep 1 to Oct 15
  - Cold-hardy, excellent erosion control
  - Overwinters, till in early spring
  
Buckwheat (Summer):
  - Plant Jun 1 to Aug 15
  - Fast-growing (6-8 weeks to flower)
  - Excellent weed suppression and pollinator attractor

Crimson Clover:
  - Plant Aug 1 to Sep 15
  - Nitrogen-fixer, beautiful spring blooms
  - Till in before going to seed

Winter Rye + Hairy Vetch (Mix):
  - Plant Sep 1 to Oct 1
  - Powerful combination: Carbon + Nitrogen
  - Excellent spring biomass

Oilseed Radish (Tillage Radish):
  - Plant Aug 15 to Sep 15
  - Deep taproot breaks compaction
  - Winter-kills, leaving organic matter

Tip: Cover crops are "green manure" - they feed the soil, not you!
